    openball: every workflow, walked in a real browser¶

    These pages describe what a person does in openball, one page per workflow, with a flow chart, the steps as a story, the screenshot of each step (taken by actually running the app, never drawn), what you should see, and what happens in the edge cases. They are written for testers, owners and anyone who needs to know what the app does, not how it is built.

    Start with the case catalogue: every case we check, for every kind of person, and how. To see how the tests sign in (and why there is no back door): LOGIN.md.
