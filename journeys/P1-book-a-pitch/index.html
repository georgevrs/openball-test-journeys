    P1 · A player books a whole pitch, pays, and cancels¶
    Who A player
    Test id P1 (three tests: happy path with cancel, 3-D Secure, declined card)
    Needs the local stack with Stripe in test mode (or, on request, the demo)
    Catalogue P-01 … P-06 in CATALOGUE.md

    What this flow is for¶

    The oldest and simplest thing openball does: book a whole pitch for an hour and pay for it. A player goes from the home page to a free hour, says who is playing, pays by card, sees the booking in «My bookings», cancels it and gets the money back. The two variants check what happens when the bank asks for extra proof (3-D Secure) and when the card is declined. Everything uses Stripe's test cards: no real money exists in this flow.

    About the card pictures: the card box is Stripe's own form, which sits in a separate frame that the screenshot does not capture. The pictures show the page around it; the digits typed are in the step text.

    What you should see¶

    • The phone field accepts a number; the contact name carries a visible test tag so a stray booking explains itself.
    • After a good payment: a confirmation with a booking reference, and the booking in «My bookings».
    • Cancelling first quotes the refund, then asks «Yes, cancel»; afterwards the booking says refunded and the database and Stripe both agree (the journey checks them).
    • A declined card gives a clear message on the card step; nothing is booked, and the same hour is still free for the next person (the journey books it again as someone else).

    Edge cases and how they behave¶

    Case Behaviour
    3-D Secure The bank's test page appears inside the page; pressing «Complete» finishes the booking
    Declined card Message on the card step; the slot stays free
    Two people want the same hour at the same instant Not walked in a browser (needs two simultaneous payers); proven in an automated API test
    Apple Pay / Google Pay Shown only on devices that offer them; not walkable here
