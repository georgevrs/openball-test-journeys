    O2 · An owner's ordinary day¶
    Who A venue owner (the demo stadium's owner)
    Test id O2
    Needs the local stack with the demo stadium seeded (the test tooling does it by default)
    Catalogue O-08 … O-14 in CATALOGUE.md

    What this flow is for¶

    After the set-up, the owner opens the console every day to see: what is booked today, who by, and with what phone number; which hours are free; how much was earned; when the money arrives. This journey is that day, and a hunt for every place an owner would look for something: it follows every link of the console and reports any promised section that is not there (as a dead end with the picture of where the owner looked), instead of stopping.

    The flow at a glance¶

    flowchart TD
    A[Sign in as the owner] --> B[Today:<br/>counts and today's hours]
    B --> C[A booked hour names who booked it<br/>and their phone]
    B --> D[Calendar by week:<br/>previous and next]
    D --> E[A free hour: «Walk-in»<br/>the hour is blocked]
    E --> F[Unblock it again]
    B --> G[Bookings list and Open games list]
    B --> H[Earnings: this week, this month<br/>takings and commission, export]
    B --> I[Payouts: state and list]
    B --> J[Venue photos: add or change]
    B --> K[Every link in the console:<br/>none is a dead end]

    What you should see¶

    • Today shows the day's counts and each hour; a booked hour carries the booker's name and phone.
    • Calendar lets the owner block a free hour as a walk-in and open it again; a committed hour cannot be disturbed.
    • Earnings show takings and the platform's commission per week and month, with an export for the accountant.
    • Payouts show each business's payout state and the payouts received.
    • The console names no section it has not built.
