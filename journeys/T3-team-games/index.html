    T3 · Team games, challenges and notifications¶
    Who A team captain (Maria) and the captain of another team who challenged her
    Test id T3
    Needs the local stack, with Stripe in test mode (accepting a challenge holds half on a test card). The matches come from the repository's sample-data seed (see below)
    Catalogue T-23 … T-32 in CATALOGUE.md
    New in #601: the «Team games» screen (answer first, day timeline) and the team-match rows in the notification inbox

    What this flow is for¶

    A captain wants to know, first, what needs their answer, and then what their team has coming up. The «Team games» screen puts the challenges other teams sent at the top, each answerable with one press, and below it a timeline of the team's matches by day, each with a state in words and an icon. The same events arrive as notifications, and the sender is told when a challenge is turned down.

    What you should see¶

    • Answer-first. The first block is the invitations («3 waiting for an answer»); each card names the challenger, the pitch, the day and time, an optional message from them, her half of the price, and two actions: accept (with the amount) or «No thanks». A countdown in words («in 33 hours», «tomorrow») says how long she has to answer.
    • My matches, by day. Each match shows the two crests (a dashed circle with «Find an opponent» when the second team is missing), the pitch and time, the price of her half, and a state that is always a word plus an icon: Deposit missing, Waiting for a second team, Booked.
    • Notifications. One unread row per challenge; marking one read lowers the counter; the challenger receives a row when a challenge is declined.
    • Teams looking for an opponent. The public matches of other teams.

    How the matches get there (a note for testers)¶

    Nothing in the screens can create one of these legacy team matches today (the create route is retired), so the journey starts with a clearly labelled test preparation step: the repository's own sample-data seed (the seed data script) is run against the test stack. It creates the account's team and ten players who join with a real invite code, saves a line-up, creates the matches and has demo teams challenge Maria's. The seed is guarded: it only runs against the walkthrough's own local database (the test helpers).

    Edge cases and how they behave¶

    Case Behaviour
    Accept a challenge (pay her half) The match page says the half is held on her card, not charged; with the Stripe test card the page then says «The pitch is yours. Your half stays held until the game»
    «A team joined your match» notification The captain who sent the challenge finds it in his inbox
    Finding With the default navigation (the demo's) the notifications page has no menu entry; the journey opens it by address. It is reachable from e-mails and with the booking navigation build: #621
    Declining a challenge on a match that was cancelled No «declined» row is written (nothing to mislead); API test
