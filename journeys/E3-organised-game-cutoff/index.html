    E3 · An organised game fills up and the cutoff confirms it¶
    Who Two players who are not in the organising group (and, behind the scenes, the group)
    Test id E3
    Needs the local stack with Stripe in test mode and a venue that can take a card
    Catalogue P-18, P-20, P-21, P-31 in CATALOGUE.md

    What this flow is for¶

    The money moment of an open game: a seat is held, not charged; at the cutoff the system decides; only then does money move. Since #606 every game in the list is an organised game, so this journey proves the moment on one: Maria's group has held its seats (see E1), the game is open, two other players hold a seat each (one with the plain test card, one with the 3-D Secure card), the deadline passes, and the real worker confirms the game and captures every hold. The database and Stripe are asked at each stage.

    (The opposite outcome, a group that never fills and a cutoff that cancels with nobody charged, is the second test of E1.)

    Step by step (from the recorded run)¶

    Step 1. Maria has organised a game with her team (see E1): every member has held a seat, so the game is open to the public with the group's seats taken and the rest free.

    on screen: SETUP (test preparation): Maria's group has started a game and every member has held

    Step 2. A player who does not know Maria opens «Games». The organised game is in the list; she opens it and sees who organises it, which seats are the group's and which are free, and «Hold my seat».

    on screen: a player who is not in the group finds the game in «Παιχνίδια» and opens it

    Same moment, in the dark theme and on a phone (390 px):

    Step 3. She presses «Hold my seat», types the Stripe test card and confirms. The page says her money is HELD, not charged («You have not been charged»); Stripe agrees: the amount is reserved, nothing is received.

    on screen: she holds a seat with the test card: held, not charged, and the database and Stripe agree

    Same moment, on a phone (390 px):

    Step 4. A second player does the same with a card that makes the bank ask for proof (3-D Secure): the bank’s test page appears, they press «Complete», and the seat is held.

    on screen: a second player holds a seat with the 3-D Secure card

    Step 5. The deadline passes (the test moves this one game’s clock a minute past the cutoff and sets its minimum to the holds that exist, instead of waiting). The system’s background job decides: enough players, so the game is ON.

    on screen: TEST HELPER (not a screen): the clock reaches the cutoff and the real worker decides

    Note: the test helpers decideNow: one UPDATE of this game’s cutoff and minimum (the minimum is set to the holds the journey has made); the stack’s own worker decides

    Step 6. The game page now says the game is confirmed and offers no seat. Behind it, the seats of the two public players were charged, and only now: Stripe shows each payment as received, for exactly the seat price.

    on screen: every hold was captured: the money moved only now

    Same moment, in the dark theme:

    What you should see¶

    • The organised game is in «Games» and says who organises it and how many seats the group holds.
    • A hold shows «You have not been charged»; in Stripe the amount is reserved, none is received.
    • After the cutoff the game page says it is confirmed and offers no seat; each public player's payment shows as received in Stripe, for exactly the seat price; the pitch is booked for the game in the database.
