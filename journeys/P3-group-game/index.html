    P3 · A group books a whole field (J-01)¶
    Who An organiser, a second organiser, and the people they invite
    Test id P3
    Needs the local stack with Stripe in test mode, captured mail (the stack's own) and the seeded booking personas
    Catalogue G-01 … G-06 in CATALOGUE.md

    What this flow is for¶

    Booking a whole field for a group is more than paying: the organiser first has to say who is coming, give the pitch a second responsible person, invite everyone, and only when everyone has answered does the money move. This journey walks the whole of it for a group of ten (the business analysis calls it J-01): gather, find the people, declare the line-up, place them on sides, name a second organiser, commit, send the invitations (which arrive as real e-mails), accept, authorise the payment and end with everybody «Kick-off ready».

    What you should see¶

    • The commit screen says plainly that no second organiser has been named yet, and why that matters.
    • All invitation e-mails really arrive (the journey reads them from the stack's captured mail, like a person reads their inbox).
    • A complete roster alone does not start the payment clock; the clock starts when everything required is in place.
    • The payment is an authorisation (a hold), never a capture, until the booking rules say so.
    • At the end the event is Booked and all ten members are «Kick-off ready»; «My games» still shows the booked journey.

    Edge cases and how they behave¶

    Case Behaviour
    A required person declines or does not answer Not walked here: it needs the 24-hour clocks to run. The rules are proven by the worker and API tests
    An invitee with no account The e-mail link leads to a token page; not walked here (see the catalogue)
    Second organiser refuses The page explains the four choices; shown in P4
