    O1 · A pitch owner gets onto openball¶
    Who A new venue owner, and the platform admin who approves them
    Test id O1
    Needs the local stack with Stripe in test mode (Stripe Connect onboarding)
    Catalogue O-01 … O-07 in CATALOGUE.md

    What this flow is for¶

    For players to find pitches, owners must put them there. This journey walks the whole road of a brand-new owner, from the public home page to a published, bookable hour that a signed-out player can see: sign up, register the business (tax number, address, phone), connect a payout account with Stripe, wait for the platform's approval, add a venue, add a pitch, set prices and publish a time and a weekly series.

    What you should see¶

    • Every call to action on the owners' page leads somewhere real.
    • Registering needs a valid tax number (ΑΦΜ, with its check digit), an address and a phone.
    • The business cannot be approved until Stripe says it can charge; the admin sees that status.
    • After approval the owner can add venues and pitches; what they publish is found by a signed-out player on the venue page and the pitch search.

    Not proven¶

    • Stripe's hosted onboarding ends with an hCaptcha that an automated browser cannot solve. The journey keeps the screenshot of that page, then a step named as the test helper finishes the test account with Stripe's documented test values and returns to the product's own return page. That step is reported NOT PROVEN by the harness, never as passed.
